Proof & Due Diligence

The information prospects need before committing

Prospective sponsors receive a verification-ready information pack relevant to the proposed scope. Only approved, current documents and evidence are shared.

What can be reviewed

  • Programme concept note and proposed implementation plan
  • Relevant governance or registration documents, where applicable
  • Safeguarding, consent and escalation approach
  • Scope-level budget and intended use of funds
  • Measurement and reporting framework
  • Pilot or field evidence as it becomes verified and available
Reporting can be agreed around
  • Participation and onboarding
  • Activity and continuity
  • Qualitative feedback
  • Fund utilisation
  • Approved stories or media

Final indicators depend on the agreed sponsorship scope and data that can be collected responsibly.

The Sponsorship Process

A clear route from interest to activation

The form starts a discussion; it does not create a financial commitment or public association.

  1. 01

    Submit an Enquiry

    Share your organisation, intended geography, area of interest and broad support level.

  2. 02

    Discovery & Fit

    TuHiTu Cares reviews programme fit, delivery readiness, priorities and mutual expectations.

  3. 03

    Proposal & Review

    A tailored scope can set out outcomes, budget, timeline, roles, safeguards and reporting.

  4. 04

    Activate & Report

    Work begins only after written agreement, internal approvals and any required due diligence.
