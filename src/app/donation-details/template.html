A Clear Giving Process

Know what happens before you pay

TuHiTu Cares collects basic donor details and then hands payment to the authorised gateway. Card, UPI PIN and online-banking credentials are entered only with that provider.

What you can expect

  • Payment credentials handled only by the authorised gateway
  • Receipt process explained before or with the donation confirmation
  • 80G information for eligible Indian donations and valid receipts
  • Programme updates and responsible reporting as applicable
Checkout currency options

  • CAD
  • AUD

Currency and recurring-payment availability depend on the configured gateway and donor location.
