Wider Community Connection

Structured Coordination

Named roles, consent, scheduling and escalation routes are agreed before launch.

Responsible Recognition

Approved facilities may be acknowledged without exposing resident identities or exploiting participation.

Operational Approval

Resident dignity comes before network growth.

Registration is not automatic approval. TuHiTu Club reviews whether the facility can support safe, consistent participation.

Minimum readiness checks

  • Valid operating registration or applicable licence
  • A named programme and safeguarding coordinator
  • A resident or authorised-representative consent process
  • Privacy, data-handling and incident-escalation readiness
  • Suitable staff support, device and connectivity for planned calls
  • Agreement on responsibilities, reporting and public communication
Funding & Support

No automatic grant or donation is promised.

Where TuHiTu Club support includes sponsored activities, devices, programme resources or financial assistance, it is discretionary, documented, purpose-linked and subject to due diligence, budget availability and monitoring.

From Registration To Launch

  1. 01

    Register

  2. 02

    Review

  3. 03

    Agree

    Define consent, roles, format, reporting and escalation.

  4. 04

    Pilot

    Start with an approved cohort and monitor the experience.

  5. 05

    Expand

    Scale only after the pilot demonstrates readiness and value.
