The Companion Programme

One trusted relationship can change the rhythm of an elder’s week.

Each approved elder is matched with one or two prepared companions who provide emotional support, social connection and something meaningful to look forward to.

“This is companionship—not caregiving.”

Companions do not replace medical, nursing, financial or emergency support. Their role is to listen, connect and show up consistently within clear programme boundaries.

Become a Companion Read about safeguarding
What companionship can include
  • Weekly phone or video calls
  • Approved in-person visits
  • Morning or evening check-ins
  • Walks and conversation
  • Reading books or the news
  • Games and shared interests
  • Help with everyday technology
  • Birthdays and festival moments
  • Accompaniment where approved
  • Simply spending quality time

From Interest to Connection

How the TuHiTu Cares journey works.

Registering is the first step—not an automatic match or approval. Every participant moves through a clear, safeguarded process.

  1. 01

    Apply or Enquire

    Choose your role and submit the relevant form on the destination page.

  2. 02

    Review & Verify

    The team reviews suitability, identity, organisation details and service context as required.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Companions complete orientation, communication, boundaries and safeguarding preparation.

  4. 04

    Match With Consent

    Connections are shaped around language, interests, availability, location and elder preference.

  5. 05

    Connect & Support

    Calls, letters, visits or activities continue with coordinator support and regular feedback.

Safety before speed.Consent, clear boundaries, a named coordinator and an escalation route come before any relationship begins.

Good to Know

Questions people ask before taking the next step.

Clear answers for volunteers, care homes, sponsors, partners and donors.

No. TuHiTu Cares is a companionship initiative. Companions do not replace caregivers, clinicians, nurses, emergency services or financial advisers.
Yes. TuHiTu Cares services and community experiences are intended to remain free for participating elders. Programme costs are supported through donations, sponsorships and volunteer participation.
The volunteer pathway is designed around consistency, typically beginning with about 30–60 minutes a week. The exact format and schedule are confirmed before matching.
Approved volunteers complete the required review, orientation and safeguarding preparation before matching. Consent, coordinator roles, connection format, boundaries and escalation routes are defined before participation begins. Read the governance details.
