A Shared-Care Model

Your organisation stays central

Partnership planning defines consent, coordination, safeguarding and the support context before connections begin.

The Partnership Model

Structured support—not an unsupervised introduction

Every partnership is planned around the elders’ needs, the organisation’s capacity and the modes of connection available in that location.

Organisation Onboarding

Confirm eligibility, appoint a coordinator and align on the programme, safeguarding and communication process.

Elder Registration

Identify interested elders through an appropriate consent-led process without exposing sensitive details publicly.

Thoughtful Matching

Matching can consider language, interests, availability, preferred format and the elder’s support context.

Ongoing Coordination

Named contacts help manage scheduling, feedback, safeguarding concerns and any required changes.

Who can enquire: Care homes Senior living communities NGOs & nonprofits Community centres Elder-support networks
Clear Responsibilities

A partnership works when both sides know their role

The detailed responsibilities are confirmed in writing for each approved implementation.

TuHiTu Club can coordinate

Programme & Companion Support

  • Programme orientation and implementation planning
  • Companion pathway, preparation and matching coordination
  • Safeguarding guidance and escalation route
  • Check-in structure and agreed progress updates
Partner organisation provides

Local Readiness & Elder Support

  • A named coordinator and timely communication
  • Appropriate elder consent and accurate non-sensitive information
  • Suitable access, scheduling or supervised space when required
  • Prompt feedback and safeguarding escalation
Partnership Readiness

Review the operating model before onboarding elders

Approved partners can review the documents and implementation information relevant to their proposed programme before agreeing to proceed.

What can be clarified in advance

  • Programme overview, eligibility and intended connection model
  • Roles, coordinator responsibilities and communication cadence
  • Safeguarding, consent and escalation process
  • Data-minimisation and information-handling expectations
  • Implementation plan and onboarding checklist
  • Feedback and reporting format appropriate to the programme
Important

No elder is matched from this website.

The enquiry form collects organisation-level information only. Personal, medical and identity-document information about elders must not be submitted here.

The Partnership Process

From registration to a safe programme start

Submitting the form records interest; partnership begins only after review, readiness checks and written confirmation.

  1. 01

    Register Interest

    Share your organisation type, location, elder cohort and preferred connection model.

  2. 02

    Eligibility & Discovery

    TuHiTu Club reviews fit, capacity, safeguarding readiness and the local delivery context.

  3. 03

    Plan & Prepare

    Both sides align on roles, consent, implementation, coordination and reporting.

  4. 04

    Onboard & Review

    Approved participation can begin in stages, with regular feedback and safeguards in place.
