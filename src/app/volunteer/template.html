A 30-Minute Weekly Commitment

Someone’s grandparent is waiting for a reason to smile. You could be that reason.

Loneliness rarely asks loudly. A familiar call, a patient listener or a shared story can give an elder something human to look forward to every week.

Apply to Be a Companion See How It Works
  • 30–60 minutes a week
  • Matched around shared interests
  • Guided and safeguarded
Simple. Personal. Consistent.

One person. One conversation. Once a week.

You do not need special expertise—just empathy, reliability and the willingness to listen.

Why This Matters

An elder’s loneliness may be quiet. It is not small.

We instinctively show up when vulnerability is visible. Older people are often easier to overlook because many do not ask for company—even when the days feel long and disconnected.

TuHiTu Cares turns concern into one simple, repeatable action: be the person who remembers to call.

If you would want someone to listen to your parent or grandparent when you could not be there, you already understand why this matters.

This is your invitation to become that person for someone else. I Can Give 30 Minutes a Week
There Is A Place For You

Be a companion in a way that fits your life

Your age, profession or location does not define your ability to care. Consistency does.

Students

Learn from lived experience, build empathy and complete meaningful service with supervision where required.

Working Professionals

Turn one small slot in your week into a steady relationship beyond deadlines and screens.

Families & Homemakers

Share conversation, culture, recipes, stories or language from home with an elder who values company.

Retirees & Community Members

Offer perspective, shared memories and peer companionship through calls, letters or local activities.

Companions under 18 participate only through an approved school, institution or guardian-supported pathway.

Choose Your Way To Connect

Start with what feels natural

Availability, location, language and the elder’s preferences shape every approved connection.

Calls & Video Calls

A regular conversation from anywhere, usually 30–60 minutes a week.

Letters & Story Exchange

Share memories, interests and encouragement at a comfortable pace.

Visits & Community Activities

Join approved in-person experiences only where local coverage and coordination exist.

Kindness With Safeguards

No one is matched just because they submit a form.

Every relationship should protect the elder, the volunteer and the care organisation supporting the connection.

Before the first connection

  • Application review and proportionate verification
  • Orientation, boundaries and safeguarding preparation
  • Consent-led matching around language, interests and availability
  • A clear coordinator and route to raise concerns or request a change
What we ask from you

Be present, respectful and reliable.

You are not expected to provide clinical, financial or crisis support. Your role is companionship within clear programme boundaries.

Care That Comes Home

When you care consistently, that care can reach your family too.

After the human reason to volunteer comes a meaningful programme benefit: eligible volunteers may nominate a parent or elder family member for a supported companionship cycle.

25 verified hours or 20 approved calls

Nominate one parent or elder family member

Eligible nominees can receive a supported 12-week companionship cycle. Calls can be coordinated across locations; in-person visits are offered only where local coverage is available.

Subject to the elder’s consent, volunteer standing, safeguarding checks, programme capacity and service coverage. No family details are collected at the application stage.
Recognition Comes After Purpose

Your service should mean something—and be recognised properly

The relationship is the reward. TuHiTu Cares also documents and recognises sustained, verified contribution.

Verified Certificate

Receive a service certificate reflecting verified hours or approved calls completed through the programme.

Milestone Goodies

Earn milestone badges and programme goodies at selected service stages, subject to availability.

Growth & Leadership

Build listening, communication and community-leadership experience, with recognition for sustained service.

Family Circle Benefit

Complete the service milestone and become eligible to nominate an elder from your own family.
